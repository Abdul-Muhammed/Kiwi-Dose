Our Story

Bringing Fresh Flavours to Mount Eden

Kiwi Dose was created with a simple vision: to bring people together through fresh flavours, creative food and warm hospitality.

Where It Began

Where Kiwi Dose Began

We wanted to create more than an ordinary café or dessert shop. Our goal was to build a welcoming destination where customers could enjoy refreshing drinks, beautiful desserts and memorable moments with family and friends.

Located in the heart of Mount Eden, Kiwi Dose combines international inspiration with the friendly and relaxed character of Aotearoa.

What We Stand For

Quality Ingredients, Memorable Experiences

Quality Ingredients

We carefully select the ingredients, fruits, sauces and toppings used across our menu.

Creative Presentation

We believe food should look as enjoyable as it tastes. Every detail is considered when preparing and presenting your order.

Friendly Hospitality

Our customers are at the centre of everything we do. We aim to provide attentive service and a welcoming experience during every visit.

Community Connection

We are proud to be part of the Mount Eden community and look forward to creating a place where local families, friends and visitors feel welcome.

Our Promise

Our Promise

Whether you are stopping by for coffee, enjoying a weekend treat or celebrating with people who matter to you, we want every Kiwi Dose experience to feel special.
