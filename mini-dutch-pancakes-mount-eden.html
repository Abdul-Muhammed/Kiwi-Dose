Mini Dutch Pancakes

Fresh Mini Dutch Pancakes in Mount Eden

What They Are

Small, Warm and Made to Order

Mini Dutch pancakes are small, round and pillowy, cooked fresh so they arrive warm and light. In the Netherlands they are known as poffertjes, and plenty of our customers simply call them baby pancakes. Whichever name you use, they are the same comforting treat.

Every serve is finished with your choice of premium sauces, fresh fruit and toppings, so you can keep it simple or build something indulgent.

Choose Your Sauces, Fruit and Toppings

Pair your poffertjes with chocolate, caramel or berry sauces, fresh strawberries and banana, crushed nuts, coconut, crumble or a scoop of ice cream.

Prefer something else warm? We also serve waffles and crêpes, and you can cool things down with an açaí bowl or a milkshake.
